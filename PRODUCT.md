# Tyler Le portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Present Tyler Le's engineering experience, education, personal project, and contact information through an interactive portfolio at https://www.tylerle.dev/.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The selected 3D gallery replaces the original homepage. Publish only this design; keep exploration alternatives outside the website repository. GitHub Pages deploys the root of main.

## Brand Commitments
Company colors and local logo assets identify each experience. Space Grotesk and DM Sans are self-hosted. Keep physical card depth, camera motion, soft shadows, and responsive reduced-motion behavior.

## Content Direction
Use high-level, factual descriptions rather than a pasted resume. Google is current: Software Engineer in AI Observability, Dec 2026 – Present. Coursera, Jan 2026 – Dec 2026, focuses on subscriptions and coupons. Amazon full-time spans Jun 2024 – Jan 2026. Group Amazon, General Atomics, and Cardea Bio internships in one card. UC San Diego is an education card. Clarity Networth is the personal project, using sanitized demo screenshots. Dates use MMM YYYY – MMM YYYY or MMM YYYY – Present. Do not invent Google achievements.

## Gallery Interaction Requirements
Exactly six cards, Google first. Clicking the active card toggles its flip; clicking a visible neighbor selects it. Card backs contain complete summaries without nested scrolling. About and Contact open within the gallery. A left profile sidebar appears only with Google, with current role, past companies, portrait, and Resume/Contact/LinkedIn links beneath the previous-company text. Keep project promotion separate from the profile. Mobile uses a compact profile above the scene and a native experience picker. The room remains full width, with fixed-size neighboring logos and staged preview/content fades. Avoid horizon lines, clipped lighting, belowfold showcases, and wide timeline bars. Keep the footer compact.

## Evidence on Hand
User-supplied resume: files/Tyler_Le_Resume.pdf. Company logo and demo screenshot provenance: images/SOURCES.md. Detailed design specification: DESIGN.md.
